How to use RushPick

RushPick turns your order receipts into one picking list, so you collect every item once and pack orders without mistakes. It works offline, with no account.

Quick start

  1. Scan or import an order receipt.
  2. Check the order and save it.
  3. Pick from the combined list.
  4. Mark each order ready when it's packed.

1Scan or import a receipt

Scan an order receipt with your camera or import a photo of one. RushPick reads the order number, customer and items right on your phone.

  • Totals, tax, payment and thank-you lines are ignored automatically.
  • The same receipt can't be added twice.
  • Receipt reading works with Latin-alphabet receipts.

For the best results, lay the receipt flat in good light and keep the whole item list in the frame.

2Check the order and save

Before saving, look over the order and fix anything the scanner misread. When you save, the order goes straight into your open batch.

3Pick from one combined list

Identical items from all orders in the batch are added up, with how many orders need each one. Collect each item once for the whole batch.

  • Sort the list by aisle or by name.
  • Tap once to mark a line as collected.
  • Enter partial amounts and weights (kg, g, L) with +/- or by typing the amount.
  • Report a shortage without changing the customer's order.
  • Open any order on its own to see what's been collected for it so far.

4Mark orders ready

Mark each order ready when it's packed. Ready and cancelled orders leave the batch, and their items come off the picking list.

5Allergies and preparation notes

Allergy notes (nuts, gluten, dairy and more) are highlighted and never merged away. For restaurants and takeaways, items with preparation notes like "no onion" stay separate on the list, so they're prepared correctly.

6Teach RushPick your product names

Match a receipt line to a product in your catalog once, and RushPick recognises it next time. The more you use it, the less you need to correct.

7Back up your data

All orders, products and receipt images stay on your device. Export a backup of your data at any time, so you don't lose it if you change phones.

8FAQ

Are receipt photos uploaded?
No. Text recognition runs on your device, and receipt photos are not uploaded.
Do I need an account or internet?
No. There's no sign-up, no login and no subscription, and RushPick works offline.
Why do I see ads?
RushPick is free and supported by ads. In the EU, UK and Switzerland, ads are shown only after you give consent, and you can change your choice in Settings.
Which languages does it support?
English, Português, Español, Français, العربية, বাংলা, हिन्दी and 中文.
